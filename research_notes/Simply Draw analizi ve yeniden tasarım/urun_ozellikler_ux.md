## 1. Launch date, platforms and status as of 2026

### Takeaway
Simply Draw (App Store ID 1639875485; Android package com.hellosimply.simplydraw) was first released on the iOS App Store on 15 Nov 2022. Wikipedia and aggregators say "launched in 2023", which likely refers to the broad or public launch. As of late September 2026 the app is active and updated very often (v1.14.1 on 27 Sep 2026). It runs on iPhone/iPad and Android phones and tablets. It has no desktop or web app, but it has a web purchase and landing funnel and a Meta AI-glasses integration. It has not been renamed or discontinued, but it has clearly been repositioned toward children and parents.

## 2. Onboarding flow, personalization, paywall placement and trial

### Takeaway
Onboarding goes: Get Started → teacher video and questions (name, age, what you'd like to draw, experience, etc.) → profile creation → free "Intro to Drawing" course (draw a fish: outline → shade → patterns) → photo of the drawing → choose a drawing path → personalized paywall. There is a 7-day free trial, but only on the yearly plan and only once per account or Apple ID. The paywall comes after the first finished drawing and path selection, so users hit it after exactly one free drawing. This is the main source of negative reviews.

### Cited Findings
- "After a short onboarding where we get to know you (like your name, age, and what kinds of things you'd love to draw), you'll create your profile and jump into your very first drawing experience: our Intro to Drawing course." In it you draw a fish: "starting with a simple outline, learning how to shade it, and finally adding your own creative patterns." "To keep learning and unlock even more exciting drawings, you'll need to subscribe." (dated 28 Jul 2026) — [Help: How soon will I get to draw something?](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/10258428-how-soon-will-i-get-to-draw-something)
- Free content is only the "Intro to Drawing" course (outlining, shading, adding patterns; subject is a fish). A subscription unlocks five structured paths, advanced skills, the personal Journal, the Playground video library and more (dated 24 May 2026) — [Help: What can I access for free](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/11111960-what-can-i-access-for-free-in-simply-draw)
- Subscribe flow: download the app → complete the free intro course → pick a drawing path → "personalized pricing" appears before any payment entry. Monthly and yearly plans are offered, and prices vary by country and platform. Android accepts card, PayPal or Google Play. Plans auto-renew — [Help: Subscribe to Simply Draw](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9256419-subscribe-to-simply-draw)

- Analysis of Simply's shared onboarding "skeleton" (dated 27 Apr 2026): brand/landing → account/profile setup with multi-profile framing → attribution question ("how did you hear about us") → identity ritual (name, signature, avatar, household type; Draw uses the signature element) → age question framed as adaptive content → motivation/goal multi-select → skill self-report or probe → practice-frequency commitment → equipment check with escape hatch → "magic demo" → value delivery before paywall → emotional completion beat → paywall with individual/family toggle, a 12-month anchor with discount %, a monthly decoy, and the free trial on the anchor plan. ### Inferences
- The paywall is triggered by intent ("choose your path" leads straight to price), which follows Simply's playbook: "The sale happens before the paywall. The paywall just collects." The value moment is one finished, shaded and decorated drawing (the fish) plus the photo "magic demo".
- Because many users are children, a paywall shown in front of kids with no parental gate produces a lot of angry reviews. A re-creation should consider a parent gate or parent-directed pricing screen. The existence of such a gate in the current app is unconfirmed.

- Whether a parental gate exists before purchase was not found.
- Whether a practice reminder or notification permission prompt exists in onboarding was not found.

## 3. Lesson and curriculum structure

### Takeaway
As of 2026 the app has three spaces. **Learn** holds an Intro course and then five drawing paths: Animals, Anime, Characters, Cute Objects and Nature. **Playground** is a leveled library of draw-along videos with "Tips & Tricks" mini-lessons, new content every 1–2 weeks. **Journal** is the photo gallery. Each path course follows a fixed rhythm: learn a skill (short video) → practice it on a subject → extra repeat drawings. iOS adds Sparks points and Playground Levels, plus "My Studio" on iPad and "Tom's Stories". Older (circa 2023–24) content included technique courses such as "Perspective Basics I", with human figures, illustrations and portraits listed as "Coming Soon" for a long time.

### Cited Findings
- "The app is divided into three spaces - Playground, Learn, and Journal." After the Intro course, the user picks a first path: Animals, Anime, Characters, Cute Objects or Nature. Course rhythm: "1. Learn a skill – short video tutorials guided by professional artists and teachers… 2. Practice with a subject – … drawing a fun character, object, or scene. 3. Repeat and grow – you'll get extra practice drawings to help make the skill stick! Every course follows this rhythm." Subscribers can switch paths with a "switch path" button (top right). The Playground unlocks after the first subject in a path. The Journal unlocks in the Intro course after the fish (dated 28 Apr 2026) — [Help: What is Simply Draw?](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/10260104-what-is-simply-draw)
- First drawing in each path: Animals → Panda; Anime → Kid Drawing; Cute Objects → Happy Popsicle; Nature → Acorn; Characters → Koala. Path switching: tap **Learn** in the left-hand menu → **Switch Path** (top right) → path selection screen. After finishing a path, users can go back and choose another. The Playground gets "fresh new drawings every couple of weeks" (dated 28 Jul 2026) — [Help: How soon will I get to draw something?](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/10258428-how-soon-will-i-get-to-draw-something)
- The Anime path is limited to English-language devices for now. Content updates arrive "every week or two" (dated 13 May 2026) — [Help: Most Common FAQs](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9256539-simply-draw-most-common-faqs)
- Tips & Tricks (inside Playground, unlocked by Sparks) is a set of short mini-lessons, each teaching one "trick" such as shading, shine, movement lines, cartoon eyes or bubble writing. Lesson template: playful intro → guided draw-along → "Now you" moment to try it alone → "What's next?" suggestion linking to a Playground drawing that uses the skill. It is reached via Playground → Tips & Tricks button on the top row (dated 9 Aug 2026) — [Help: Tips & Tricks](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/15046234-tips-tricks-playground)
- Tom's Stories: short "storytime" episodes of about 1–1.5 minutes in which teacher Tom tells personal, kid-friendly stories, each ending with a simple drawing idea. A "Stories" button appears in the side menu. It unlocks one week after starting a subscription, with a tooltip when a new story appears. Only "a few episodes" exist (dated 17 Aug 2026) — [Help: Tom's Stories](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/15055217-what-is-tom-s-stories)
- My Studio: a "cozy drawing studio" that unlocks automatically in the sidebar after enough Sparks. It has personalization unlocks, animations and interactions, and a collection of drawings with staged step-by-step guidance plus decorating the finished creation. iPad only, and English must be among the device languages (dated 11 Aug 2026) — [Help: What is My Studio?](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/14636876-what-is-my-studio)
- Framework taught: "Shape → Shading → Texture". Techniques named include blending, shading and texture. Practice subjects include fruits and animals. Users are encouraged to draw everyday objects at home (dated 10 Dec 2024) — [Help: Independent artist](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/10258504-how-does-simply-draw-help-me-to-become-an-independent-artist)
- Older content (reviews, undated, "since launch in 2023"): courses on nature (tree, mushroom, sailboat, cabin), animals and human figures. Many advanced topics (human figures, illustrations, portraits) showed "Coming Soon" for 1–3 years. A bug, "Oh No! Unknown screen type: Course", blocked "Perspective Basics I" — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1639875485/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/reviews)
- A third-party summary says the app "launched with one course available in 2023 and two courses available in 2024". This is low-reliability aggregator text (research.com via search snippet; page returned 403) — [Research.com](https://research.com/software/reviews/simplydraw-review)
- Store description: "New drawing sessions are added weekly." — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485). Topics mentioned by a Jan 2025 reviewer: nature, animals, cartoons. Lessons are "short and sweet", with toggleable ambient background music and "over 50 hours of lessons" — [Making Everyday Magic](https://makingeverydaymagic.com/app-based-art-review/)
- An aggregator claims a "library of over 100 tutorials" (unverified) — [search snippet, Research.com/aggregators](https://research.com/software/reviews/simplydraw-review)

### Inferences
- Learn is a linear, gated progression. Each path step unlocks separately ("each path step is unlocked separately and doesn't use sparks"). Playground is the open-ended, leveled practice space. This mirrors Simply Piano's split between "Courses/Paths" and "Songs library".
- One lesson ("drawing") is probably 10–20 minutes of video-guided drawing, but no official lesson length was found.

### Gaps
- I found no official count of courses, lessons or drawings per path, and no lesson durations.
- No daily plan, streak or daily challenge feature is documented in any 2026 help article or review. I cannot confirm streaks exist in Draw (unlike Simply Piano).
- The full historical adult-course list (for example, all "Perspective Basics" and "shading" courses) and when it was replaced by the 5-path structure are unknown.

## 4. Pedagogical approach (instruction format, medium, feedback, progress, instructors)

### Takeaway
The app teaches with pre-recorded, step-by-step video from real art teachers. Each drawing is split into small steps, and the video pauses at each step so the learner can catch up and tap "Next". Drawing is done only with pencil on physical paper, never on screen. Early marketing and third parties emphasized AI photo feedback that grades drawings against the reference. 2026 help docs describe the photo mainly as a way to save to the Journal. Feedback is framed as positive and confidence-building, including 1–2-day encouraging replies from the teacher "Tom" (iOS/English). Progress is measured through path completion, Sparks and Levels, not through accuracy scores, as far as documented.

### Cited Findings
- "Simply Draw exclusively uses traditional pencil and paper methods", emphasizing "skills you can use every day, everywhere in real life" — [Help: Most Common FAQs](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9256539-simply-draw-most-common-faqs)
- "step-by-step video tutorials created by professional artists and teachers… breaking drawing into simple, easy-to-follow steps" — [Help: What is Simply Draw?](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/10260104-what-is-simply-draw). Store text says to learn "what interests you" through "easy-to-follow video tutorials made by professional artists and teachers" with "insightful tips and tricks" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
- Kids can use it alone if they can follow "simple on-screen instructions independently (like tapping 'Next' to move forward)". The photo step now serves mainly as a completion ritual, portfolio entry and motivation. Evaluative AI grading, if it still exists, appears only in the onboarding demo and is not presented to users as corrective feedback.
- The "teacher reply in 1–2 days" with uniformly positive messages suggests templated or batch-generated responses (possibly AI-assisted). This is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- It is unconfirmed whether AI grading still runs in the 2026 lesson flow, and what the grading UI looked like (score, stars, overlay comparison).
- I found no full instructor roster, names beyond "Tom", or credentials.
- No documented skill-assessment or adaptive-difficulty logic beyond path choice.

## 5. Key screens and interaction patterns

### Takeaway
Navigation is a left-hand sidebar menu (Learn, Playground, Journal, plus Stories and My Studio when unlocked), with the avatar in the top-left for profiles and settings. The lesson player is a video with step pauses, a tap-to-open settings wheel (video language) and Next progression. After a drawing: camera capture → save to Journal. Gamification is Sparks points, Playground Levels with locked drawings, stickers such as "Drawn Together", and unlockable My Studio. There is no public community; the product is explicitly "no strangers". Sharing is external: a time-lapse via Meta glasses and teacher-send.

## 6. Required materials

### Takeaway
The app needs only a pencil, paper and an eraser (help article dated 2026), plus the device, ideally propped up so the learner can watch while drawing. It does not use a stylus or on-screen drawing. The device's camera is used to photograph results. The onboarding includes an "equipment check with escape hatch" step.
