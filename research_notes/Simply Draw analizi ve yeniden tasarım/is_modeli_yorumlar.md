# Simply Draw (Simply Ltd, formerly JoyTunes): Business Model, Pricing, Market Performance and User Sentiment

Research date: 2026-09-28. Store data (ratings, ranks, IAP lists) was fetched on this date unless marked otherwise. Several data sources (Sensor Tower, AppMagic, AppBrain, Research.com, Dun's 100, Meta Ad Library, TikTok, Reddit) blocked or returned empty pages; those gaps are listed explicitly.

## 1. Pricing and monetization (tiers, prices USD/EUR/TRY, trial, bundles, paywall, cancellation)

### Takeaway
Simply Draw is a hard-paywall subscription app. You get one free intro lesson (draw a fish), then a monthly or yearly "Simply Draw Premium" plan that covers up to 5 profiles. US in-app prices run from $9.99 to $119.99, and Turkey's from ₺349.99 to ₺6,999.99. The app is also included in Simply's cross-app Family Plan (Piano + Guitar + Sing + Draw). Almost all negative sentiment comes from this pricing design: an abrupt paywall, a "free" download that reads as a bait-and-switch, and trial/cancellation friction.

### Cited Findings
- **Free content = one intro course only.** The free "Intro to Drawing" lesson (you draw a fish) has to be completed before subscription options appear. Monthly and yearly plans exist, and the article says "subscriptions vary in cost depending on your country and the platform". — [Simply Help Center: Subscribe to Simply Draw](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9256419-subscribe-to-simply-draw)
- **Auto-renewal:** "All subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel the auto-renewal". Monthly plans renew on the same date each month, and yearly plans take the full fee upfront. The help article gives no refund policy for Draw. The support contact is draw@hellosimply.com or in-app chat. — [Simply Help Center](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9256419-subscribe-to-simply-draw)
- **Payment channels:** Apple App Store on iOS. On Android: card, PayPal or Google Play, depending on country. — [Simply Help Center](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9256419-subscribe-to-simply-draw)
- **US App Store IAP list (fetched 2026-09-28):** $9.99, several $14.99 tiers, $59.99, $89.99, $119.99. A reviewer quoted on the page mentions a membership range of "$7.99–$119.99". — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
  - Inference from the price points: roughly $9.99–14.99/month and $59.99–119.99/year. The different annual tiers are likely A/B-tested or discounted intro prices.
- **A review snippet in search results** describes pricing as "approximately $7.99 per month for a 12-month commitment" (about $96/year billed annually). This came from an aggregator summary and was not verified verbatim. — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485?see-all=reviews&platform=ipad)
- **Turkey App Store IAP list (fetched 2026-09-28), all named "Simply Draw Premium":** ₺349.99, ₺399.99 (x2), ₺599.99, ₺799.99, ₺2,299.99, ₺3,499.99, ₺4,999.99, ₺6,999.99. — [App Store TR](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
  - Inference: roughly ₺349.99–799.99 for monthly tiers and ₺2,299.99–6,999.99 for annual tiers. The wide spread suggests price tests or intro offers. A ₺6,999.99/year tier is very expensive relative to Turkish incomes, which is a pricing gap a competitor could exploit.
- **Relative price perception:** reviewers say the Draw subscription costs "almost twice the price of Simply Piano". This comes from a search summary of store reviews and is a user claim, not verified. — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485?see-all=reviews&platform=ipad)
- **Profiles:** Simply Draw subscriptions support "up to 5 profiles". A standalone Draw plan "only gives you access to Simply Draw". Individual Piano or Guitar plans do not include Draw unless upgraded to the Family Plan. — [Simply Help Center: Draw with other subscriptions](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9330596-how-to-use-simply-draw-with-my-other-simply-subscriptions)
- **Family Plan bundle:** it includes Simply Draw and all Simply apps. Users log in with the same email, and Settings shows "Premium Account". — [Simply Help Center](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9330596-how-to-use-simply-draw-with-my-other-simply-subscriptions)
- **Family Plan price (third-party, 2025–2026):** about $23.90/month or $209.90/year, up to 5 profiles across Piano, Guitar, Sing and Draw, with a 14-day free trial. It is described as roughly $40/year more than the individual Piano plan. — [Pianoers Simply Piano review](https://pianoers.com/simply-piano-review-the-honest-truth-about-learning-piano-with-an-app/); [Groupon Simply Piano](https://www.groupon.com/coupons/simply-piano) (this comes from search summaries of these pages and has not been checked against Simply's own checkout)
- **Homeschool reviewer (Jan 31, 2025):** about $100/year for five family accounts (about $20 per child per year), a free trial, and "over 50 hours of lessons". — [Making Everyday Magic, App Based Art Review](https://makingeverydaymagic.com/app-based-art-review/)
- **Trial and cancellation complaints (Simply-wide, Trustpilot, TrustScore 2.6/5 from 8 reviews):**
  - "Offered a 7 day trial and charged £89.99 immediately. Refused refund" (Apr 15, 2026)
  - Charged for "monthly and annual subscription plus songbook" despite cancelling the trial (Jan 7, 2026)
  - "They tried charging €189 on my account" (Jun 7, 2026)
  - Cancellation "extremely difficult" and not possible from a PC (Jun 27, 2025)
  - "There is no way to cancel this" (Sep 23, 2022)
  - Positive counterexample: "Refund within 20 minutes" (Sep 30, 2025)
  - Most of these reviews concern Piano, not Draw specifically.
  - Source: [Trustpilot hellosimply.com](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hellosimply.com)
- **EUR price signal:** a €189 charge is mentioned on Trustpilot (Jun 2026). The review does not say which product it was for. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hellosimply.com)

### Inferences
- The business model is a web-and-app subscription funnel with price localization and heavy price testing (9 TRY tiers and 6+ USD tiers under one product name).
- The value proposition of the 5-profile plan is aimed at families and parents, not solo adults.
- Using the Family Plan as an upsell for Draw means Draw partly exists to raise the value of Simply's bundle for households that already pay for Piano.
- The biggest gap for a competitor is a paywall that does not feel like a trick. A meaningful free tier (several lessons or a full beginner course), prices shown up front before any drawing starts, one-tap cancellation, and fair regional pricing (especially in Turkey) all address the dominant complaint.

### Gaps
- No official USD, EUR or TRY price card mapping each IAP tier to monthly or annual plans. The onboarding page (join-draw.hellosimply.com) did not render.
- No confirmed trial length for standalone Draw. Only the 7-day trial (Simply-wide, Trustpilot) and the 14-day trial (Family Plan, third party) are sourced.
- The Google Play IAP price range was not retrieved because the Play page fetch was truncated.
- No Draw-specific refund policy found. A Piano refund article exists but was not fetched.

## 2. Market data (downloads, revenue, ratings, rankings, Simply Ltd company overview 2026)

### Takeaway
Simply Draw is a mid-sized hit, not a blockbuster:
- **Google Play:** 5M+ downloads, 4.71★ from about 140K ratings.
- **US App Store:** 4.7★ from about 785K ratings, #49 in Education (Sep 28, 2026).
- **Turkey App Store:** 4.6★, #86 in Education. The TR rating count is uncertain.

The ratings are inflated by a known pattern: children leave 5★ reviews "so you can see this" while actually complaining. Simply Ltd is an Israeli unicorn (valued at $1B in 2021) with about $200M in estimated 2024 revenue and roughly 220–240 employees. I found no Draw-specific revenue figure and no evidence of 2025–2026 layoffs.

### Cited Findings
- **US App Store (fetched 2026-09-28):**
  - 4.7/5 from 785K ratings; #49 in Education; age rating 4+; 112.9 MB; iOS 15.2+.
  - Languages: English only.
  - Latest version 1.14.1, released the same day. Release notes are a generic "erased the bugs" message.
  - Source: [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
  - The 785K count is corroborated by an aggregator that analyzed 749,895 reviews. — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1639875485/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/reviews)
- **Turkey App Store (fetched 2026-09-28):**
  - 4.6/5; #86 in Education; English only (no Turkish localization).
  - The rating count was parsed as "30,000", but the Turkish storefront abbreviates numbers ("B" = bin/thousand), so the real count may be 3K or 30K. Treat it as unverified.
  - Source: [App Store TR](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
- **Google Play:** 5,000,000+ downloads (AppBrain listing, via search snippet) and 4.71/5 from about 140 thousand ratings (search snippet). — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/com.hellosimply.simplydraw); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hellosimply.simplydraw)

  - Several "Premium Unlocked" MOD APKs exist (v1.5.7, 1.7.0, 1.9.3), which signals demand for piracy of the paywalled content. — [Platinmods](https://platinmods.com/threads/simply-draw-learn-to-draw-ver-1-9-3-b444-mod-apk-premium-unlocked.303169/)

  - The App Store ID (1639875485) points to a listing created around mid-2022, so there was likely a soft launch before 2023. That is my inference.

  - HQ in Tel Aviv. CEO Yuval Kaminka; co-founders Yigal Kaminka and Roey Izkovsky.
  - Rebranded from JoyTunes to Simply in July 2022.
  - Products: Simply Piano (2017), Guitar, Sing, Draw, Tune (free) and Piano XR (Vision Pro, Dec 2024; Android XR, 2025).
  - Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_(software_company))
  - The Wikipedia fetch said the company was "founded 2017", which conflicts with its own funding history (a Series A in Aug 2014). JoyTunes was founded well before 2017, so treat 2017 as the Simply Piano launch year, not the company's founding.

  - About $97M raised in total: $5M Series A (2014, Aleph), $25M (2019, Qumra), $50M (2021, GV, Qualcomm Ventures, Hearst Ventures).

  - About $200M in 2024, an estimate from Latka, which also cites about $1.1M revenue per employee.

### Inferences
- Compared with Simply Piano (tens of millions of downloads), Draw is a secondary product line. It is probably a small single-digit percentage of Simply's roughly $200M revenue, but this is unverified.
- The US App Store rank of #49 in Education (Sep 2026) indicates steady but not top-tier grossing or downloads.
- An English-only app in Turkey reaching #86 in Education implies real latent demand in Turkey. A Turkish-localized competitor with TRY-appropriate pricing has an open lane.
- The 4.7★ average overstates satisfaction. Many "5★" reviews are complaints written by kids (see section 4), so the average should not be read as product-market fit.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower, data.ai or AppMagic download or revenue estimate for Draw specifically. Those pages were not reachable, and no public snippet was found.
- No per-country Google Play ratings, and no Education category rank history.
- No 2025 or 2026 disclosed revenue or valuation update for Simply, and no statement of strategy for Draw (for example, AI feedback investment or the size of the content team).
- Dun's 100 and PitchBook profiles blocked the fetch (403 or paywall).

## 3. Marketing and acquisition (ad creatives, audience, positioning)

### Takeaway
Store positioning targets absolute beginners with "step-by-step" pencil-on-paper video lessons by professional artists, a "customized learning journey", feedback on your work, and "new sessions weekly". In practice the audience splits between adult beginners and children or families: the 4+ rating, the 5 profiles, and homeschool reviewers all point to kids. The kid audience is exactly where the paywall backlash is loudest. I could not access ad-library evidence.

### Cited Findings
- **Store claims:** "step by step drawing lessons", a customized learning journey ("learn to draw what interests you"), draw-along video tutorials "made by professional artists and teachers", feedback on your work "to help you evolve as a creator", "new drawing sessions added weekly", and an emphasis on teaching skills rather than copying. — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
- **Medium:** pencil drawings on paper. The app uses the camera to photograph your drawing, which is how it gives feedback. — [App Store listing via search summary](https://apps.apple.com/gt/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485?l=en-GB&platform=ipad); [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1639875485/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/reviews) (reports of forced photo uploads and camera glitches)
- **Homeschool and parent positioning:**
  - A reviewer calls it "a no-brainer for any homeschool family", recommends it for ages 7+, and says kids can work independently.
  - Content categories: Nature, Animals, Cartoons. Optional ambient music.
  - Source: [Making Everyday Magic (Jan 31, 2025)](https://makingeverydaymagic.com/app-based-art-review/)
- **Facebook presence:** the Simply Draw page (@hellosimplydraw) has about 2,187 followers, which suggests paid acquisition rather than organic community. — [Facebook: Simply Draw](https://www.facebook.com/hellosimplydraw/)
- **Other channels:** Simply Draw ads appear inside other mobile games and apps, and TikTok hosts a "Simply Draw Ad" discovery page. The content could not be retrieved. — [TikTok discover: Simply Draw Ad](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/simply-draw-ad)
- **Web onboarding funnel:** join-draw.hellosimply.com ("Simply Draw | Start Learning") exists. The page is JS-rendered and did not return content. — [join-draw.hellosimply.com](https://join-draw.hellosimply.com/)

### Inferences
- Simply is probably re-using the Simply Piano playbook: performance marketing on Meta, TikTok and in-app ad networks, a web quiz funnel, and an intro-price annual plan.
- The in-game ad placements likely explain the large number of child users who download the app, then hit the paywall and leave angry 5★/1★ reviews.
- Positioning gap: the product promises "feedback", but users say it does not deliver (section 4). A competitor with real, visible AI feedback on the user's own drawing would differentiate on Simply's weakest claim.

### Gaps
- No Meta Ad Library or TikTok Creative Center creatives were retrieved. Both need interactive or JS access. The number of active ads, the hooks used, and the countries targeted (including Turkey) are unknown.
- No explicit statement from Simply on target audience splits (adults vs. kids).

## 4. User reviews: what users love and hate (themes, quotes, frequency)

### Takeaway
- **Praise:** calm, high-quality step-by-step video lessons that give real beginners real results ("hang on the fridge"), and a good fit for kids and homeschool use.
- **Complaints, by rough frequency:**
  1. The paywall arrives after one free lesson and feels deceptive. This is dominant, and especially common among kids and in Turkish reviews.
  2. Price is too high.
  3. Too little content, with courses "coming soon" for months or years.
  4. The promised feedback is missing or weak.
  5. Camera and photo-upload bugs block progress.
  6. Trial, billing and cancellation friction (Simply-wide).
  7. Experienced users find it too basic and rigid.

### Cited Findings

**Aggregate sentiment**
- JustUseApp's sentiment analysis of the reviews: 66.7% negative and 33.3% positive. Paywall frustration is "the most consistent grievance", with users saying the app "doesn't warn you at the start". — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1639875485/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/reviews)
  - Caveat: the methodology is opaque, and the score conflicts with the 4.7★ store average.

**Theme A: paywall after the first lesson feels deceptive (most frequent)**
- Of the 10 most recent reviews visible on the US App Store reviews page (Apr–Sep 2026), about 8 are paywall complaints rated 5★ "so you can see this":
  - "I hate paying" (5★, Sep 2026): "I only put five stars just so you can see this this app is terrible…"
  - "READ THIS!" (Sep 19, 2026): "I love this app but like, why do we have to pay…"
  - "It's terrible I only gave this 5 for u to see it" (Aug 12, 2026): "I could only draw fish and I wanted to make my mom happy…"
  - "Fake app" (Jun 17, 2026): "I downloaded this app thinking I'm gonna draw for free…"
  - Source: [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485?see-all=reviews&platform=ipad)
- The US store listing's review summary says users complete the free lessons "like drawing a fish" but cannot continue without buying a membership. — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
- Search summaries of reviews add that only "a single drawing" is allowed before payment, that it is "not worth downloading unless you are prepared to pay almost immediately", and that "constant subscription prompts interrupt the experience". — [App Store reviews, India storefront](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485?see-all=reviews&platform=ipad)

**Theme A in Turkey (all visible TR reviews are negative about payment)**
- "PARALI ÇOK KÖTÜ" (paid, very bad), Jan 1: criticizes limited free content.
- "Kazık" (rip-off), 03/01/2025: payment requested mid-session after instruction.
- "Yalan!" (lie!), 19/05/2025: misleading "free" claim; free online alternatives are better.
- "I REALY HATE THISA APP", 09/03/2025: "HER ŞEY PARALI" (everything is paid).
- Source: [App Store TR](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)

**Theme B: price too high**
- Draw is "very expensive, costing almost twice the price of Simply Piano" (user claim in a search summary). — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485?see-all=reviews&platform=ipad)
- On Trustpilot, a Feb 8, 2026 review says Simply "targets children with 'free' download; £100/year". — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hellosimply.com)

**Theme C: content runs out or is too thin (a churn and retention indicator)**
- Users report only 2–3 courses available after more than a year, with content marked "coming soon" indefinitely. One reviewer says they waited "3 years" for locked skills. — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1639875485/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/reviews)
- The app focuses on certain genres at a time ("Nature and Animals currently available as courses"), and users want more variety. — [Google Play listing reviews via search summary](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hellosimply.simplydraw)
- The store claims "new drawing sessions added weekly". — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
  - Users' perception of thin content contradicts this claim.

**Theme D: feedback and AI promise not delivered**
- A US reviewer says the app doesn't deliver the advertised AI feedback. — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)

**Theme E: bugs and camera friction**
- Reported issues: camera-requirement glitches, an "Unknown screen type: Course" error, and forced photo uploads that block progression. — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1639875485/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/reviews)
- Every recent release note is the same generic "erased the bugs" text. — [App Store US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)

**Theme F: billing, trial and cancellation (Simply-wide)**
- See the Trustpilot quotes in section 1: immediate charges on "7-day trials", duplicate subscriptions, hard cancellation, and refusal of refunds. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hellosimply.com)

**Theme G: too basic or rigid for non-beginners**
- The app assumes no prior drawing experience and makes users draw practice subjects they don't want. Reviewers see it as good for beginners and limiting for experienced artists. — [Google Play reviews via search summary](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hellosimply.simplydraw)

**Positives**
- Store and creator review: "Simply Draw makes creating art a very calming and fun experience!" — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hellosimply.simplydraw)
- Praise for the step-by-step teaching style, a patient instructor tone, and realistic results. One user made drawings good enough to "hang on the fridge". — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1639875485/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/reviews)
- "SUPER FRIENDLY AND NICE" (Jun 7, 2026): "Taught my kids how to draw quick and fun". — [App Store US reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485?see-all=reviews&platform=ipad)
- Users call it "the best drawing app" and say it is "really helping me with perspective"; some discovered a talent for drawing. — [App Store reviews via search summary](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-draw-learn-to-draw/id1639875485)
- Homeschool reviewer: "professional and easy to follow" lessons; "short and sweet" videos; kids can work independently. — [Making Everyday Magic (Jan 31, 2025)](https://makingeverydaymagic.com/app-based-art-review/)

**Approximate frequency of themes (my estimate from the sampled reviews, not a statistical count)**

| Theme | Share of negative mentions | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A. Paywall / "free" feels deceptive | ~60–70% | Dominant in both US and TR samples; heavily kids |
| B. Price too high | ~15–20% | Often combined with A |
| C. Content thin / "coming soon" | ~5–10% | Appears among longer-term users; a churn signal |
| D, E. Feedback missing, bugs, camera | ~5–10% | |

- Positives mostly concern lesson quality, calm tone, beginner results and kid-friendliness.

### Inferences
- **Paid users are satisfied with lesson quality. Dissatisfaction is concentrated at the paywall and in content depth.** The two main gaps a competitor can target:
  1. A generous, transparent free tier with honest "free" messaging, especially for kids and parents.
  2. A deep, continuously growing curriculum across fundamentals, genres and levels, so paying users don't run out.
- **Real feedback is a defensible differentiator.** Simply advertises feedback, but users report that it is missing or weak. Per-stroke or per-photo AI critique with concrete corrections would target that gap.
- **Kids are a large, unmonetized, angry segment.** A parent-gated purchase flow, kid-safe free content, and a family plan priced per child could turn this segment around.
- **Turkey:** there is no Turkish localization, every visible TR review is a pricing complaint, and the TR price ladder runs up to ₺6,999.99. A Turkish-language app with TRY-appropriate pricing (or a low-cost or ad-supported tier) is a clear opening.
- **Piracy signal:** "Premium Unlocked" MOD APKs show that demand exists at a price point below Simply's.

### Gaps
- Reddit (r/learntodraw, r/ArtistLounge, r/SimplyPiano) threads on Simply Draw did not surface in search, and I found no citable Reddit discussion.
- YouTube review comments were not retrieved.
- Google Play review texts, dates and developer replies could not be extracted because the page was truncated.
- There is no rigorous frequency count. The theme percentages above are estimates from about 20 sampled reviews plus aggregator summaries.
- There are no public retention or churn metrics (D30 retention, trial conversion, renewal rates) for Simply Draw. The only churn evidence is qualitative: "content running out" and "coming soon" complaints.
